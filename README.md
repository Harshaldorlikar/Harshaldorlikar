### The Approach

I have been building software exclusively with AI since November 2022. I do not write code by hand. I describe what I want, and I work with AI until it exists. A single person operating this way can ship at the speed of a full engineering team. 

The bottleneck is no longer the AI. It is the prompt. 

I lived this specification gap hundreds of times. Every project started the same way: I described what I wanted, the AI built something close but wrong, and I spent hours correcting assumptions I didn't know I was making.

That is why I am building Kosmo.

### Selected Work

**[Kosmo](https://askkosmo.com)**
An AI prompt compiler. It turns raw ideas into structured, tool-aware prompts grounded in live documentation. It automatically injects expert guardrails, strict boundaries, and hidden reasoning loops to eliminate prompt babysitting.

**[A-41 Housing](https://a41housing.com)**
A premium real estate web app built to market and lease a 3BHK student housing property. It fully booked the property before physical renovations were even completed.

**[GOAT](https://github.com/Harshaldorlikar/Goat/)**
An autonomous sports betting AI agent. Built to test the limits of AI autonomy in a high-stakes, unpredictable environment. It successfully turned $5 into $71 in 4 days, and then lost it all. A perfect case study in risk management.

**[EdAI](https://youtu.be/4cnpDlm0wxw)**
A micro interactive learning platform that turns static textbooks into game-like courses to force active recall.
